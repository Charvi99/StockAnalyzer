# 🧭 Top 5 Swing Trading Strategies (Pro Edition)

## 1️⃣ The Breakout + Retest Strategy (Bread & Butter)
**Concept:** Price breaks a well-defined resistance, then retests it as new support — a classic continuation setup.

**Setup:**
- **Pattern:** Ascending triangle, flat-top range, or tight flag near resistance.
- **Indicators:**
  - Volume surge on breakout
  - 20 EMA crossing above 50 EMA
  - RSI > 55 (not overbought yet)
- **Entry:** On retest candle with bullish rejection wick.
- **Stop Loss:** 1–2% below the retest low.
- **Take Profit:** 2–3× stop distance or next resistance level.

**Example:**
```
Stock: NVDA
Resistance: $125
Breakout: $127 with 150% avg volume
Retest Entry: $125.5
Stop: $122.5
Target: $131.5–$134
```

## 2️⃣ RSI Divergence Swing (Reversal Setup)
**Concept:** Price makes a new low, but RSI shows a higher low — momentum exhaustion and reversal hint.

**Setup:**
- **Pattern:** Double bottom or falling wedge.
- **Indicators:**
  - RSI divergence (price ↓, RSI ↑)
  - Bullish engulfing candle
  - MACD histogram moving toward zero
- **Entry:** Close above previous swing high.
- **Stop Loss:** Below recent swing low.
- **Take Profit:** 1.5–2.5× risk or 50 EMA.

**Example:**
```
Stock: AMD
Low 1: $88 (RSI 28)
Low 2: $85 (RSI 34)
Entry: $90 breakout
Stop: $84
Target: $102–$106
```

---

## 3️⃣ Moving Average Pullback (Trend Continuation)
**Concept:** Buy dips within an existing uptrend — safest and most consistent swing setup.

**Setup:**
- **Pattern:** Higher highs and higher lows.
- **Indicators:**
  - Price bounces off 20 or 50 EMA
  - RSI between 40–60 on pullback
  - Volume dries up on pullback, rises on bounce
- **Entry:** Bullish candle closing above EMA.
- **Stop Loss:** 1 ATR below swing low.
- **Take Profit:** Previous swing high or 2× risk.

**Example:**
```
Stock: AAPL
Trend: Up above 20 EMA
Pullback: $220 → $214
Entry: $216.5
Stop: $213
Target: $223–$226
```

---

## 4️⃣ Earnings & Dividend Gap Strategy (Fundamental Catalyst)
**Concept:** Trade corporate event gaps (earnings/dividends) for volatility.

**Setup:**
- **Pattern:** Gap-up on strong earnings or dividend.
- **Indicators:**
  - Volume ≥ 200% average
  - Gap holds above resistance
  - RSI > 60
- **Entry:** 15–30 min pullback or next-day retest.
- **Stop Loss:** Below gap support.
- **Take Profit:** 2–3× stop or next daily resistance.

**Example:**
```
Stock: MSFT
Earnings Gap: $390 → $410
Retest Entry: $404
Stop: $397
Target: $425–$430
```

---

## 5️⃣ Bollinger Band Squeeze (Volatility Expansion)
**Concept:** Periods of low volatility precede explosive moves.

**Setup:**
- **Pattern:** Tight consolidation, contracting bands.
- **Indicators:**
  - Bollinger Band Width near 6-month low
  - MACD crossover forming
  - Volume increase on breakout
- **Entry:** Candle close outside upper/lower band.
- **Stop Loss:** Inside opposite band or 1 ATR.
- **Take Profit:** 2–3× risk or opposite band.

**Example:**
```
Stock: TSLA
Range: $240–$248
Breakout: $250 close, bands expanding
Entry: $251
Stop: $246
Target: $263–$268
```

---

# ⚙️ Stop-Loss / Take-Profit Formula
**ATR-based approach:**
```
Stop = Entry − (1.2 × ATR)
Target = Entry + (2.5 × ATR)
```

- Risk ≤ 1% per trade
- Journal every setup
- Avoid major macro events (CPI, FOMC)
- Stack confluences: pattern + volume + RSI + news
